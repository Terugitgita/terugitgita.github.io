Privacy Policy

AI Buddy Make (“the App”) / Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Our approach

The App stores your answers and self-portrait only on your device. There is no developer server, and the developer never receives your data. Section 3 explains what Google receives in order to show ads.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your answers to questions, self-portrait scores, values you adjusted by hand, and answer history
  • Optional attributes you enter: sex, date of birth, birth time (time of day), country/region of birth, blood type, job type, industry, whether you hold a managerial role, country of residence, whether you have a spouse/partner, number of children, and whether you work or study
  • Rooms and themes (layout, owned items, unlocked themes), point records (including how many ads you watched), Brain Workout records, and app settings
  • Character looks and names (chosen from suggestions, or a name of up to 12 characters that you type)

There are no fields for your own name, address, employer, email address, or phone number. Please do not enter a real person’s name or personal information as a character name either.

3. Data sent outside your device

The App connects to the internet only for in-app purchases and ads. It does not include analytics or crash-reporting tools.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The App shows non-personalized ads only from Google AdMob. Ads appear when you choose “Watch an ad for points,” and between Brain Workout games (not shown to users who bought the main features).
  • The App does not ask for tracking permission and does not use the advertising ID (such as IDFA).
  • Your answers, self-portrait, attributes, export files, points, character names, and Brain Workout records are never given to the ad network.
  • When an ad is shown, information that the operating system and Google’s ad SDK attach automatically (device type, OS, language, IP address, app information, etc.) is sent to Google. It is used to prevent ad fraud and to count impressions. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, Google’s consent screen asks for your consent regarding ads. You can change your choice in Settings.
  • If your date of birth shows that you are under 16, ad requests are tagged as coming from a user under the age of consent (your date of birth itself is not sent).

4. Files you export

  • Your profile for AI: created only when you export it, and handed over through your device’s share sheet. Once you give it to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another service, it is handled under that service’s terms and privacy policy. Your country/region of birth is never included. You can choose which attributes to include before exporting. Character names are included.
  • Backup: created only when you export it. If you set a password, it is encrypted (AES-256-GCM). It does not include items bought with money.
  • Diagnostic log: created only when you export it. It contains only error types and screen locations — no answers or attributes.

5. Device features

  • Notifications: scheduled on your device only if you turn on the “Today’s 3” reminder (off by default).
  • Biometrics: used only if you turn on App Lock. Authentication is handled by your device’s OS; the App never receives it.
  • Vibration: used as feedback for actions. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Location is not used. The weather and night sky outside the room are simulated from the date and your device’s time zone.

6. In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Apple or Google. The App only receives which products (main features, deep-dive packs, themes, etc.) you bought.

7. Deleting data

“Erase all data” in Settings deletes the data stored on your device (only the record of purchased products remains, so you don’t have to buy them again). Deleting the App removes all data on the device, including purchase records. You can get purchases back with “Restore purchases.”

8. Age

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. Ads for users under 16 are handled as described in section 3.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.

10. Contact

Please use GitHub Issues. Posts there are public, so do not include personal information such as your name, email address, or answers.
